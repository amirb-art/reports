📐 Statistical Methodology & Calculation Principles:
• Strict 7-Day Window: Every daily measurement is benchmarked strictly against the prior 7 daily observations (window size = 7), eliminating multi-month catalog skew and adapting dynamically to natural exponential growth.
• Rolling Z-Score: Computed as Z = (Δtoday - μ7d) / σ7d.
• Normal Variance Zone (Green): Fluctuations within ±1.0 standard deviation (±1σ).
• Moderate Volatility Zone (Orange): Values between 1.0σ and 2.0σ.
• Critical Anomaly Threshold (Red): Deviations exceeding ±3.0 standard deviations (|Z| > 3.0σ) or reporting freezes (Flatlines with Δ=0..1 following high variance). Triggers LOW Confidence status in AI intelligence reporting.
